# Pictopia Photobooth — business profile

*Single source of truth for site content. Fact IDs refer to `sources.md`.*

## Who they are
Pictopia Photobooth is a small, owner-run event photobooth rental in Central Luzon. The owners are Juliette Barrera and Kenneth David (brief, F20). They work from two bases: **Brgy. Estipona, Pura, Tarlac** and **Magalang, Pampanga** (F6, F7). The Facebook handle includes "2025", and the brand was refreshed in late September 2026 with a new cartoon-camera logo (F23, F24). It is a young business, but a busy one. In the last fortnight alone it posted a wedding, a combined 1st birthday and christening, an 18th birthday (debut) and a church anniversary (F21).

## What they sell
Photobooth service at events. Guests take unlimited-style photo sessions, prints come out on the spot, and the full photo set goes up as an album afterwards (F21, F22).

**Their own booking form lists these options (F13–F16):**
- **Booth type:** Mirror Photobooth, or Traditional Photobooth (live monitor view)
- **Print display:** standee frame, or full magnetic board
- **Print layout:** 4x6 horizontal, 4x6 vertical, classic photo strip, Polaroid-style
- **Nice-to-haves:** customised welcome screen, customised backdrop, customised printed props

**Pricing:** ₱3,500–₱6,000 per event (brief, F19). We don't yet know how hours, booth type and add-ons affect the price. A "book now and get up to ₱2,000 discount" promo runs on their site (F18).

## Who buys
From the brief, plus confirmation in their event posts:
- **Couples:** newly engaged and weddings
- **Parents:** 1st birthdays, christenings (often held together, as in the Sept 26 event), kids' parties
- **Debutantes and their families:** 18th birthdays, a major Filipino milestone
- **Organisations:** corporate events, church and community anniversaries
- **Trade partners:** caterers, event stylists and coordinators who bundle or refer suppliers

These customers are planning a family celebration, often at a resort or pavilion venue such as Tagpuan sa Nayon in Pura. They are price-conscious and want to know three things: is the date free, how much is it, and what will the prints look like.

## How they differ
- **Price:** ₱3,500–₱6,000 sits below the regional vendors listed at ₱4,500–₱7,500+ and well below Manila rates of about ₱7,500 for 2 hours (C5, C6).
- **Two booth styles** at that price, including a mirror booth, which competitors sell as premium (C1).
- **Covers both Tarlac and Pampanga** from bases in each province.
- **Visible volume:** each event gets a full photo album posted within days.

## Proof available

## Brand
The logo is a chunky cartoon camera in golden yellow, orange and cream with dark brown outlines and "splash" burst marks. The wordmark is a heavy rounded white "PICTOPIA" in a yellow outline, over "PHOTOBOOTH". The feel is playful, sticker-like and warm, which suits family parties more than luxury weddings. An older cover graphic uses brown and tan.

The voice in their posts is warm and celebratory ("Congratulations to the newlyweds…", "Happy 1st Birthday & Christening…").

## Current web presence — what's lacking
- **Google Site (S2):** a single page with only a promo banner and a "Book now" button. There is no description, photos, packages, prices, service area or contact details. The logo is a tiny generic icon.
- **Booking (S3):** a Google Form with no prices, no date or venue field visible in the summary, and no confirmation of availability. Customers have to message to find out the price.
- **Phone numbers are inconsistent:** three different mobile numbers appear across the page, cover and form (F2–F4).
- **No reviews collected** on Facebook, and no Google Business Profile found.
- **Not findable:** searches for "Pictopia Photobooth Tarlac" return competitors and a Jakarta business of the same name, not them.

## Competitors (summary — see competitors.md)
The Memoria Booth (Tarlac) is the regional leader: 6.1K followers, mirror, high-angle and vintage booths, video guestbook, online booking and bridal-expo awards. KN Photo & Mirror Booth (since 2019), TYL Unlimited and EG23 are other local Facebook-only operators. **None of them has a real website with live pricing and date booking.** That gap is Pictopia's opening.
